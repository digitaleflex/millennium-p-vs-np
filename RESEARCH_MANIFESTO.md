# Research Manifesto

## Mission

Study P vs NP with scientific discipline, reproducibility and intellectual honesty.

## Central question

Does P = NP?

Equivalently: can every decision problem whose proposed solution can be verified in polynomial time also be solved in polynomial time?

## Principles

### Rigor before novelty
A familiar result correctly understood is more valuable than an apparently novel but invalid argument.

### Evidence is not proof
Experiments can reveal patterns, contradictions and useful examples. They do not establish universal asymptotic complexity claims.

### Falsifiability
Every research claim should expose what could prove it wrong.

### Barrier awareness
Before proposing a major proof strategy, investigate known barriers including relativization, natural proofs and algebrization.

### Reproducibility
Experiments must record inputs, versions, parameters, hardware and reproduction procedures.

### AI transparency
AI-assisted reasoning must be identified and independently checked.

### Negative results matter
A failed proof attempt can be a useful research artifact when its failure is precisely documented.
