# Known Results

This document will collect established results relevant to P vs NP, with precise statements and primary or authoritative references.

Initial baseline:

- P ⊆ NP.
- SAT is NP-complete.
- A polynomial-time algorithm for any NP-complete problem would imply P = NP.
- A proof of P ≠ NP requires a genuine complexity-theoretic lower-bound argument, not merely poor observed performance.
