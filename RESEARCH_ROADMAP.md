# Research Roadmap

## M0 — Research Infrastructure
Repository architecture, taxonomy, templates, citation policy, journal and reproducibility rules.

## M1 — Mathematical Foundations
Logic, sets, functions, proof techniques, algorithms, Turing machines, asymptotic analysis.

## M2 — Complexity Theory
Time and space complexity, P, NP, co-NP, reductions, completeness and related classes.

## M3 — NP-Completeness
SAT, Cook-Levin, 3-SAT, CLIQUE, Vertex Cover, Independent Set, Hamiltonian Cycle, Subset Sum and TSP.

## M4 — Complexity Landscape
Study relationships among P, NP, co-NP, PH, PSPACE, EXP, BPP and related classes.

## M5 — Lower Bounds
Study techniques for proving computational lower bounds and their limitations.

## M6 — Barriers
Relativization, Natural Proofs, Algebrization and later barriers discovered in the literature.

## M7 — Computational Research
Reproducible experiments involving SAT, graph problems, reductions, algorithms and scaling.

## M8 — Research Hypotheses
Develop and test original hypotheses only after sufficient grounding in prior work.

## M9 — Proof Attempts
Formal paper-style proof attempts with explicit assumptions, lemmas and verification.

## M10 — Formal Verification
Formalize selected mathematics using proof assistants where useful.

## M11 — Original Research
Only claims surviving prior-art review, counterexample search and independent verification enter this stage.
