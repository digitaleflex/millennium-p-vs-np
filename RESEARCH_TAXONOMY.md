# Research Taxonomy

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| KNOWN | Established result from reliable literature. |
| DEFINITION | Formal definition used by the project. |
| HYPOTHESIS | Provisional explanatory or research claim. |
| EXPERIMENT | Computational investigation. |
| OBSERVATION | Empirical observation without universal proof. |
| CONJECTURE | Mathematical claim not yet proved. |
| LEMMA | Proposed intermediate mathematical statement. |
| PROOF | Complete mathematical demonstration. |
| COUNTEREXAMPLE | Construction refuting a claim. |
| RESULT | Validated research result. |

## Rule

Moving from one status to another requires explicit evidence and review. In particular, experiments cannot directly become proofs, and AI-generated arguments cannot be accepted as proofs without independent verification.
